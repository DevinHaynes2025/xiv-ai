# Africa Business Network

Africa is a first-class region. It is **not** one market.

**Status:** PROTOTYPE (landing + country catalog) · NOT CONFIGURED (statistics)

## Model

Country-level configuration is authoritative. Regional grouping (North, West, Central, East, Southern Africa) is for discovery and intelligence only. Shared legal rules are not implied.

All recognized African UN member states are configured with ISO codes, well-known official languages, currencies, and time zones. Industries, trade connections, risk scores, and companies stay empty until sourced.

Future discovery experiences (hub, supply chain, startups, manufacturing, logistics, agriculture, energy, FinTech, healthcare, trade) are named in configuration. Dozens of screens were not built.
