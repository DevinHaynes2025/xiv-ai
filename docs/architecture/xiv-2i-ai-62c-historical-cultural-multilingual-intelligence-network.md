## Mission

Build XIV's **Civilization Intelligence Layer**: a governed knowledge architecture allowing **authorized** XIV agents to reason across human history, languages, cultures, professions, locations, and time periods while preserving provenance, uncertainty, security, and modern context.

**User story:** As an XIV AI user, I want authorized agents to understand historical knowledge, professional disciplines, languages, cultural contexts, and changing conditions so that XIV can reason with greater context while clearly distinguishing verified facts, historical records, interpretations, beliefs, uncertainty, and predictions.

## 19. Historical simulation boundary

Historical information may support scenario reasoning. It **must not** become deterministic prediction.

Allowed: “Similar historical conditions produced several different outcomes.”

**HISTORICAL ANALOG ≠ FUTURE GUARANTEE. SIMULATION ≠ REALITY.**
