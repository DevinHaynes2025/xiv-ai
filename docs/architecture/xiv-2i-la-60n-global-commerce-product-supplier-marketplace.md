## 1. MISSION

Connect XIV Business Intelligence OS to commercial lifecycle end-to-end. Unify PHYSICAL + INFORMATION + TECHNOLOGY + COMMERCIAL supply chains under governed, evidence-labeled contracts. Docs-only this commit.

## 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Commerce systems collapse discovery into approval, quote into contract, forecast into inventory, scan into truth, ETA into guarantee, listing into endorsement, AI summary into review, recommendation into purchase, and simulation into production action. LA-60N separates analysis from authority.

## 3. NON-GOALS (this commit)

No runtime commerce engine; no LIVE Amazon/Walmart; no autonomous PO/payment/price/shipment/customs/machine control; no L4; no invented PASS; no full LA-60O–60W docs.

## 4. FOUR SUPPLY CHAIN PLANES

PHYSICAL (goods/movement), INFORMATION (sources/decisions), TECHNOLOGY (systems/dependencies), COMMERCIAL (offers/orders/settlements). Connected ≠ trusted across planes.

## 5. CORE LIFECYCLE CONTRACT

Lifecycle stages are first-class nodes with temporal edges. Each stage transition requires labeled evidence and authority class. Event ≠ authorization.

## 6. AUTHORITY CLASSES

L0 observe → L1 recommend → L2 draft → L3 human-gated execute → L4 autonomous (**DISABLED**). Agent consensus never elevates authority class.

## 7. EVIDENCE LABELING

Every commercial claim carries provenance: OBSERVED / INFERRED / VERIFIED / UNKNOWN / CONTRADICTED. Inferred must be labeled. Unknown is valid.

## 8. GlobalProductGraphV100

Canonical product identity graph: SKUs, variants, components, substitutes, categories, markets. **PRODUCT GRAPH EDGE ≠ FACT.** Temporal edges; CURRENT ≠ HISTORICAL.

## 9. PRODUCT GRAPH EDGE STATES

FACT / CLAIM / INFERRED / HYPOTHESIS / DEPRECATED. Inferred edges cannot silently promote to FACT.

## 10. PRODUCT IDENTITY RULES

Same name ≠ same product. Product ID ≠ ownership. Marketplace SKU ≠ passport identity unless linked with verified mapping.

## 11. GlobalProductPassportV100

Versioned product passport: materials, origin claims, certifications references, images classes, authenticity claims, history links. **PRODUCT PASSPORT ≠ LEGAL CERTIFICATION.**

## 12. PASSPORT VERSIONING

Every passport mutation creates a version. Corrections are append-only. Silent rewrite prohibited. Historical passport ≠ current passport.

## 13. PRODUCT ID ≠ OWNERSHIP

Holding a product ID, listing, or passport reference does not confer ownership, title, or disposition rights.

## 14. PRODUCT IMAGE CLASSES

REAL_PHOTO / SUPPLIER_PROVIDED / MARKETPLACE / AI_GENERATED / UNKNOWN. **AI PRODUCT IMAGE ≠ PRODUCT EVIDENCE.**

## 15. ProductAuthenticityEngineV100

Scores authenticity claims from hashes, documents, supply evidence, marketplace signals. **HASH ≠ AUTHENTICITY. AUTHENTICITY CLAIM ≠ CERTAINTY.**

## 16. HASH / CONTENT ADDRESSING

Content hashes prove bit-identity of artifacts, not authenticity of goods, ownership, or legal certification.

## 17. ProductHistoryGraph

Temporal product events: design, sourcing, manufacture claims, shipment, sale, return, recall signals. History ≠ live state.

## 18. Product Time Machine

Replay product state as-of time T with revision memory. What was known then ≠ later revised knowledge.

## 19. ProductRecallBrain

Detects and ranks recall-like signals from sources. **RECALL SIGNAL ≠ OFFICIAL RECALL.** Official recall requires verified authority source.

## 20. SupplierIdentityGraphV100

Supplier entities, aliases, locations, ownership claims, relationship edges. Verification states: DISCOVERED / CANDIDATE / VERIFIED / APPROVED / SUSPENDED / UNKNOWN.

## 21. SUPPLIER DISCOVERED ≠ APPROVED

Discovery and graph presence never imply purchasing approval.

## 22. SUPPLIER VERIFIED ≠ ENDORSED

Verification of identity/documents ≠ XIV endorsement of quality, ethics, or fitness.

## 23. SupplierPassportV100

Versioned supplier dossier: capabilities, certifications references, risk summaries, conversation history links, quality graph links. Passport ≠ contract.

## 24. SupplierDiscoveryBrainV100

Authorized / licensed / public sources only. No unrestricted scraping. Publicly reachable ≠ free to copy / train / republish.

## 25. SUPPLIER MATCHING

## 26. SupplierRiskBrain

## 30. RFQ LIFECYCLE STATES
