# XIV Vision

XIV AI is a security-first Business Intelligence Network and Modular Business OS.

**One World. One Platform. Infinite Opportunities.**

Connect companies, employees, consumers, entrepreneurs, software systems, and governed AI agents through private organizational Universes — without forcing companies to rip out the software they already use.

## Pillars
- Private Universes (tenant isolation)
- Governed AI agents with human approval gates
- Story Engine (explain what changed and why)
- Role experiences: Consumer, Anonymous Employee, Business/Executive, Entrepreneur/Builder
- Security is the product (Zero Trust, least privilege, audit)
- Model-agnostic, cloud-agnostic, local-first development

## Moat
Trust model, context graph, workflows, and permissioned intelligence — not dependence on one model vendor or one cloud.

## Horizon
Build Version 0.1 like a disciplined startup: prove Universe isolation, business understanding, Story Engine, agent coordination, and the consumer innovation loop before owning heavy infrastructure.
